The problem

Matcha is easy to love and hard to buy well.

01

Too many grades

Ceremonial, premium and culinary matcha run from about $1 to over $3 a serving, with no guide to what fits what you want.

02

Tins go stale

Scoops, whisks and clumps at home. Powder fades once the tin is open, and it does not travel well.

03

Café habits add up

A café matcha builds the habit, but cost and quality swing from cup to cup, and the habit does not come home with you.

How it works

One goal. Twenty sticks. A card that tells you how.

Every box is built around the goal you chose. You open it, follow the card, and drink one stick a day.

  1. 1

    Take the quiz

    Five questions on your goal, taste, caffeine tolerance and when you like to drink it.

  2. 2

    Get matched

    You receive one goal line, picked for you. You can swap to another goal at any time.

  3. 3

    Open two boxes

    Each month, one mailer holds two boxes of 10 single-serve sticks, 2 grams each, plus your ritual card.

  4. 4

    Drink one a day

    Tear, shake or whisk, then drink. Twenty days of ritual, and the rest of the month is yours.

Questions

The short answers.

Does matcha contain caffeine?

Yes. Matcha has natural caffeine, so the Energy, Skin and Active routine lines contain it. Hojicha is lower in caffeine, and the botanical blend in the Sleep and recovery line is caffeine-free.

Will this help me lose weight?

We do not make weight-loss claims. The Active routine line is built to support an active routine, and nothing more.

How is it shipped?

Both boxes come in one mailer each month, labeled "Days 1 to 15" and "Days 16 to 30," so you are never waiting on a second package. We ship within the US first, with Canada planned later.

Can I change my goal or pause?

Yes. You can skip a month, pause, or swap your goal at any time from your account.

What if I do not like a stick?

Tell us which goal and taste you want next. Swapping goals is the fastest fix, and we will adjust your next box.
